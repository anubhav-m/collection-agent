Fictional policy for Maple Grove Example Credit, a made-up lender in a demo simulation.

## Who can ask for hardship help

A borrower who has had a job loss, a large drop in income, a serious illness or a
family emergency can ask for hardship help. Hardship requests are always reviewed by a
member of our team, not by the chat assistant.

## What a hardship review can offer

After a review, a team member may offer a short pause of up to 60 days, a lower
payment for up to 6 months, or a longer plan. Late fees are waived while a hardship
review is open. No option is approved until a team member confirms it in writing.

## What to have ready

Have a short description of what changed and when. The team member may ask for a
recent statement of income. You will never be asked for a password or a card PIN.
