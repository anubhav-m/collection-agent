Fictional policy shared by the made-up lenders in this demo simulation.

## Choosing how we contact you

A borrower can choose to be contacted by email, text message, phone, or mail, and can
pick more than one. Contact happens only between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m. in the borrower's
local time.

## Asking us to stop

A borrower can ask us to stop contacting them at any time. The chat assistant passes
the request to a member of our team, who confirms it in writing. After that, we only
send letters that we are required to send.

## Changing details

Phone numbers, email addresses and mailing addresses are changed by a team member
after identity checks. The chat assistant cannot change contact details.
