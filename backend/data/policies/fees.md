Fictional policy for Riverbend Sample Finance, a made-up lender in a demo simulation.

## Late fees

A late fee of $15.00 is charged when a payment is more than 10 days late. Only one
late fee is charged per missed payment, and no late fee is charged on an account in
an active payment plan as long as plan payments are made on time.

## Returned payment fees

A fee of $10.00 is charged when a bank transfer or check is returned unpaid. No fee
is charged for a declined debit card payment.

## Fees we never charge

There is no fee to set up a payment plan, to pay early, to pay in full, or to use
the chat assistant.
