Fictional policy shared by the made-up lenders in this demo simulation.

## Accepted ways to pay

Payments can be made by bank transfer from a checking or savings account, by debit
card, or by mailed check or money order. Credit cards and cash are not accepted.

## Automatic payments

A payment plan can be paid automatically from a bank account on each due date. A
borrower can turn automatic payments on or off at any time, up to 3 business days
before a due date.

## When a payment counts

Bank transfers and debit card payments made before 5 p.m. Eastern time count on the
same business day. Mailed payments count on the day they arrive. A receipt is sent
by email for every payment.
