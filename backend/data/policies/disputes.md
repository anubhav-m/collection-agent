Fictional policy shared by the made-up lenders in this demo simulation.

## How to dispute

A borrower who believes a debt is not theirs, or that the amount is wrong, can
dispute it in chat, by mail or by email. The chat assistant passes every dispute to a
member of our team and does not discuss payments on a disputed account.

## What happens next

Collection activity on the disputed account pauses while the dispute is reviewed. A
team member sends a written answer within 30 days, with the records used to decide.

## Identity theft

If a borrower says the account was opened by someone else, the team member will
explain how to file an identity theft report. The borrower does not need to pay
while that review is open.
